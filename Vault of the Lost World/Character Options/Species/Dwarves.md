# Abilities
**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (about 4-5 feet tall)  
**Speed:** 30 feet

**Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.
**Dwarven Resilience.** You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
**Dwarven Toughness.** Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.
# Subspecies
## Hill Dwarf 
^Hill
#PlayersHandbook 
#### Hill Dwarf Abilities
**Stonecunning.** As a Bonus Action, you gain Tremorsense with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You must be on a stone surface or touching a stone surface to use this Tremorsense. The stone can be natural or worked.

You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
#### Hill Dwarf Locations
You can find hill dwarfs across large grasslands, dug into hills where they work more with wood and stone than metal
## Mountain Dwarf 
^Mountain
#AdventurersGuideToTheLostWorld
#### Mountain Dwarf Abilities
**Rock Climber.** You have a climb speed equal to your walking speed
**Earthshaper.** You know the *[[Mold Earth]]* cantrip, and you can alter the spell so that you can affect a 10ft cube within 60ft of you, an amount of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a long rest
#### Mountain Dwarf Locations
Mountain dwarfs dig deep into the bases of mountains, with their 2 large factions being in the west US and in the Himalayas

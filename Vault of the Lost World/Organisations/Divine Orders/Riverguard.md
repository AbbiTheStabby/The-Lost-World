An order dedicated around the ferryman [[Serjiu]], dedicated to helping him track all those who cross back into the mortal realm without his knowledge. While they permit resurrection spells as those involved Serjiu guiding the souls back to the land of the living, Necromancy is forbidden by them as it is seen as bringing unauthorised souls back to life without watching for the consequences of such actions

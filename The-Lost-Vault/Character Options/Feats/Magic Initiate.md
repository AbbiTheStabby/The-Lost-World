**Repeatable.** When you select this feat (not when it is granted through a background) you may choose to learn from the [[Magic Initiate#^Wizard|Wizard]], [[Magic Initiate#^Cleric|Cleric]], or [[Magic Initiate#^Druid|Druid]] spell lists. You may take this feat multiple times, choosing a different spell list each time

## Magic Initiate - Wizard
^Wizard
**Two Cantrips.** You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Wizard spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat)
**Level 1 Spell.** Choose a level 1 spell from the Wizard spell list. You always have that spell prepared. You can case it without using a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have
**Spell Change.** Whenever you gain a new level, you can replace one of the spells you chose for this feat with a different spell of the same level from the Wizard spell list

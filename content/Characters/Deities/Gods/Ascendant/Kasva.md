## Story of Kasva's Seeds
As a mortal, Kasva was known for her plant transplanting, her ability to combine plants to create new ones, however her most well known and impactful ones were the seeds of life & death. These seeds would create a plant that bore two fruits, a [[Fruit of Life]] and a [[Fruit of Death]], that could heal you or kill you. The fruit of life could cure any illness, magical or non-magical, and was rumoured to be able to bring someone back from the dead or even give a spirit physical form. The fruit of death on the other hand would end your life, and may even be able to kill the physical form of Gods (Although they may be able to regenerate given enough [[Laws of Divine Intervention|Devotion]]). 

Kasva did not abuse her powers, as she went around assisting the people of the [[Yimeal Desert]] with agriculture in the arid environment, actions that gave her the following to enter godhood.

There was a time when a wealthy business man travelled to the desert, having heard of Kasva's powers, alongside his sickly friend. This man enjoyed his life but recently had caught a dangerous illness that threatened himself, so he planned to ask for a seed to grow the plant, with his sickly, almost dead friend there to accept the fruit of death. When he arrived at [[Temple of Seeds|Kasva's Temple]] he asked Kasva for a plant and so she planted a seed in the temple's soil floor, and using her staff she grew it to mature, its two identical fruits hanging in front of the two visitors. The sickly man reached for a fruit, ate of it, and collapsed, coughing on the floor when his wealthy friend reached for the other and took a large bite, tasting the legendary fruit as he keeled over, dead before his body reached the floor. The now-healthy friend stood up and turned to Kasva

"Why did you not tell him that was the wrong fruit? I was ready to die, he was not?"

The goddess replied coldly "Those who cling to death, live, and those who cling to life, die"

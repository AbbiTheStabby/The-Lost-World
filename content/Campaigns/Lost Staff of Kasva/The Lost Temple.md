###### Section 1: The Entrance Cave
A few creatures are feeding on the carcass of a dead giant hyena, while also hiding in this cave from the desert sun. The creatures don't fully trust each other but would happily team up for a chance at food (such as what they did to the hyena, and will do to the party)

| Amount of Players | Creatures                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 3                 | Ogre                               |
| 4                 | Ogre, Giant Vulture                |
| 5                 | Ogre, 2 Giant Vultures             |
| 6                 | 2 Ogrillon Ogres, 2 Giant Vultures |
The Door to the temple is unlocked but rusted shut, any attack / damage / oil will open it
###### Section 2: The Entrance Hall
The entrance hall holds 5 doors (Aside from the entrance) that lead to other rooms, and are all unlocked aside from the large stone one leading into section 7. The desks around the walls of the room have old papers covered in drawings of plants & seeds, and writing explaining how they are grown. This information can be sold to any agriculturist for 5GP, but a merchant may buy it (To resell) for 3GP.

###### Section 3: The Barracks
The barracks holds 11 Beds, with 6 skeletons in total. The Skeletons hold `dice: 1d6` Silver pieces each (Total: `dice: 6d6`), However one of their corpses has an intact holy symbol of [[Kasva]] (It looks like a silver sheaf of wheat)

###### Section 4: The Farmlands
This is a small farming room that is open to the sky with a clear roof, however this roof is mostly covered by sand, letting little light through and isn't visible from the surface. The dirt is dry and arid and lacks any nutrients.

At the far end of the room there sits a shrine to Kasva, adorned with used incense and empty candles

###### Section 5: The Storeroom
This is a room covered with crates. One one side sits mouldy food crates, all the food is expired but there are open crates of bread, potatoes, and various spices (Such as chillies). Other crates on this side hold 3Kg of Seeds, 1Kg still being good seeds the others have died by now (Merchants will pay 5cp/Kg, but if they find out about the duds they wont pay for them)

One the other side sit crates of equipment, including:
- 5 Small Copper sheafs of wheat (Non-magical)
- 2 Lengths of Rope
- 2 Potions of Healing (Minor)
- 3 Full Waterskins
- 1 Flagon of Holy Water

###### Section 6: The Kitchen
This is a small Kitchen / Eating area big enough to seat 8 people at once across the tables. there is a small shrine to Kasva in a corner of the room, where a small idol of the goddess made out of silver with agate eyes stands, perfectly clean (This is a luck stone / stone of good fortune).

###### Section 7: The Prayer Room
The door to the prayer room is sealed, and can be unlocked through magic or by inserting something into the keyhole shaped like a sheaf of wheat. Inside the room there is a mummy behind the altar

On the wall behind the altar there is a depression behind to store items, with a small inset square big enough for a divine idol. There is a section of the back wall that is fairly obviously meant to slide back, and if the players press on the inset square is goes down. The players need to place the idol found earlier onto the square for it to move backwards. Alternatively the players can Flick a lever located under the bench of the altar, and this opens the passageway to section 8

###### Section 8: The Vault
The vault has been undisturbed for much longer than the rest of the temple, and has various piles of gold and other items. There is approximately:
- `dice: 12d8` worth of copper coins
- `dice: 10d8` worth of silver coins
- `dice: 8d8` worth of gold coins
There is also a stand holding up the lost [[Staff of Kasva]], with plants growing from the cracks in the floor trying to reach the staff

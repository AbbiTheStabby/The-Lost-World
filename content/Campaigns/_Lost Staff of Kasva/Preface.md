The players meet up outside a tomb located in the [[Yimeal Desert]], fight through it, and at the end find the (lost, now found) [[Staff of Kasva]]. However when they go to a nearby outpost to sell the rest of their loot a man trudges through the city as he is looking for the staff, to exact revenge on the goddess [[Kasva]] as he is a descendant of the wealthy man in the story of her seeds.

The players goal then is to trek through the Yimeal to find the [[Temple of Seeds]], and return her staff. However the priest that collects the staff has been working with the man and now has the staff. The players can fight him off and ultimately find the chosen of the goddess and give them the staff, completing the campaign (maybe even with the rich man bursting in and trying (and failing) to fight an archmage)

The plan is for the campaign to start at 3 but you could start them at 1 with a trek to the tomb

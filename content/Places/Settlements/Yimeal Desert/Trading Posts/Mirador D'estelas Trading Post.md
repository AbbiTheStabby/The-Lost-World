**Owners:** The Mirador D'estelas family, a family of desert elves known for their stargazing and worshipping of [[Lady Starlight]], as this trading post & its oasis sit atop a raised rock with brilliant views of the night sky and land around them

**Location:** The outpost sits as the second stop along [[The Dead Road]] if coming from the east

**Shops:**
- [[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^Supplies|Mirador D'estelas Supplies Store]]
- [[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^Bazaar|Mirador D'estelas Bazaar]]
## Shops In Depth
## Mirador D'estelas Supplies Store
^Supplies
A supplies store that offers rations, water, and other survival equipment at a minor markup, accepts a [[Mark of Dead Passage]] in exchange for a tenday's worth of supplies. The shop is ran by [[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^Father|(Father) Mirador D'estelas]], the mayor of this trading post 

The ropemaker [[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^RopeSalesman|Marques Travidor]] has made a home here, selling desert-made rope to travellers to use when traversing the rocky nature of the area. His ropes sometimes make it out of the post by trader and are sold off as "Yimeal Rope"

### Services for Sale
#### Lodging
[[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^Father|(Father) Mirador D'estelas]] offers lodging at his outpost for 1sp a night, or bundled in with a mark of passage.

## Mirador D'estelas Bazaar
^Bazaar
The Bazaar of this outpost, where scavengers attempt to sell items to traders that pass through

### Marques Travidor
^RopeSalesman
A Darker Skinned [[Elves#^Sea|Sea Elf]] who came from the oceanside town of [[Coastal City]] because of his girlfriend, now wife, [[Mirador D'estelas Trading Post#^Daughter|(Daughter) Mirador D'estelas]]. He is a talented ropemaker who wants to take his wife away from her parents business, perhaps to his hometown or just a better city to raise their future children. She is not against this but her father keeps finding reason for them to stay.

The Scribes are an order of librarians & historian worshippers of [[Sehltir]] who seek to record all the events that occur in order to preserve their knowledge for future generations.
## Renown with the Scribes
### Earning Renown
- Gifting lost historical artefacts / records to the scribes
### Benefits of Renown

| Renown Score | Title     | Rewards                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3+           | Friend    | You are welcome at any shrine to Sehltir, and are offered a poor lifestyle while there assuming you assist around the shrine / temple                                                                      |
| 5+           | Scribe    | You can ask for assistance with any arcana, history, or religion checks while at a temple, gaining advantage, as the scribes search the archives                                                           |
| 10+          | Devotee   | The priests will gladly cast any divination spell up to level 3 for you, for free assuming you have the material components                                                                                |
| 15+          | Librarian | You may make a request for knowledge from the [[Library of Sehltir]], acting as a casting of the [[Legend Lore]] spell, however once you have used this you cannot use it again till your renown increases |

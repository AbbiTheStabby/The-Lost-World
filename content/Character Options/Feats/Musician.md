You gain the following benefits.
- **Instrument Training.** You gain proficiency with three #MusicalIntrument of your choice
- **Encouraging Song.** As you finish a Short or Long rest, you can play a song on a #MusicalIntrument with witch you have proficiency and give [[Heroic Inspiration]] to allies who hear the song. The number of allies you can affect this way equals your [[Proficiency Bonus]]

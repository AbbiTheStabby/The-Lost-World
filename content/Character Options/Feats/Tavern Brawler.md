You gain the following benefits.
- **Enhanced Unarmed Strike.** When you hit with your [[Unarmed Strike]] and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an unarmed strike
- **Damage Rerolls.** Whenever you roll a damage die from your unarmed strike, you can reroll the dice if it rolls a 1, and must use the new roll
- **Improvised Weaponry.** You have proficiency with [[Improvised Weapons]]
- **Push.** When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike as part of the [[Attack Action]] on your turn, you can deal damage to the target and also push it 5 feet ($1\frac{1}{2}$ metres) away from you. You can only use this benefit once per turn

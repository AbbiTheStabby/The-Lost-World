**Difficulty: $\star \star \star$**

*Bring to powers of Tarot to life*
While diviners will use a tarot deck to read the future, you use the cards to enact it, calling upon the powers of [[The Dealer]] to scry devastation on your foes. You may have always admired the tricks of charlatans, or perhaps worship the dealer himself.
## Level 3: Magic of the Cards
You have a magical tarot deck that you use to activate various abilities, a pool of **Deck Energy** that expands as you level up. Any ability that requires a saving throw uses your Charisma DC (8 + [[Proficiency Bonus]] + Charisma Bonus). The **Card Die** you use for abilities also expands as you level up

| Rogue Level | Die Size | Deck Energy |
| ----------- | -------- | ----------- |
| 3           | D6       | 4           |
| 5           | D8       | 6           |
| 9           | D8       | 8           |
| 11          | D10      | 8           |
| 13          | D10      | 10          |
| 17          | D12      | 12          |

You recharge one deck energy on a short rest, and regain all deck energy when you finish a long rest.

You may expend **Deck Energy** to activate the abilities below, or others you gain from this subclass.
- **Flames:** Spend 1 **Deck energy** to conjure a well of flames 10ft in diameter, and make all creatures inside the well take a dexterity saving throw or take 2 rolls of your **Card Die** worth of Fire Damage, or half as much on a successful save
- **Donjon:** You may expend 1 **Deck Energy** to cast Hold person at level 2. Once you have reached level 9 you may expend 2 **Deck Energy** to instead cast hold monster
- **Skull:** You may expend 1 **Deck Energy**. If you do, select a creature within 18m and force them to make a constitution saving throw. If they fail they take 4 rolls of your **Card Dice** worth of Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice) or half as much on a successful save
## Level 3: Performing Cards
You gain proficiency with both [[Playing Card|Playing Cards]] (Or another #GamingSet  if you are proficient) and Performance (Or another skill if you are already proficient)

## Level 3: Lethal Cards
You know how to use a deck of cards as a dangerous weapon, and can activate and use the *Talon* card as a weapon with the following traits:

**Weapon Category:** Simple Melee  
**Damage on a Hit:** **Card Dice** Slashing plus the ability modifier used for the attack roll  
**Properties:** Finesse, Light  
**Mastery:** Vex (you can use this property, and it doesn't count against the number of properties you can use with Weapon Mastery)

## Level 9: Tricks of the Trade
You have become so proficient in card magic that you have learnt to cast some cards without any cost or drain on your body. The cards below have no **Deck Energy** cost:
- **Key:** You cast the [[Knock]] spell
- **Comet:** You can use the comet card as a projectile with the following traits:
	**Weapon Category:** Simple Ranged  
	**Damage on a Hit:** **Card Dice** Bludgeoning plus the ability modifier used for the attack roll  
	**Properties:** Ranged (9m / 36m)
	**Mastery:** Vex (you can use this property, and it doesn't count against the number of properties you can use with Weapon Mastery)
- **Sage:** You cast the [[Legend Lore]] spell. You can only use this card once per long rest
## Level 13: Cards of Many Things

The following cards are added to your deck and can be cast with **Deck Energy**
- **Gem:** You can expend 2 **Deck Energy** to cast the [[Fabricate]] spell
- **Ruin:** You may expend up to 3 **Deck Energy**. You then create a 9m * (1.5m times the energy spent in this activation) line of shaking rubble. All creatures in this line must make a strength check or land prone, Taking 3 rolls of your **Card Dice** on a failure or half on a success

## Level 13: Deck of Powerful Things
You may choose a card to specialise in, gaining the ability to activate that card alongside other boons
### Jester & Fool
You specialise in the cards of luck, Jester and Fool. Whenever a creature within 9m of you makes a [[D20 Test]], You may expend 1 **Deck Energy** to either impose advantage (With the Jester Card) or disadvantage (With the Fool Card)
### Sun
You can activate the sun card, causing it to glow with bright light in a 6m radius and dim light 6m beyond that, or you can channel it, expending 2 **Deck Energy** for the following effects (Centred on the card)
- You create 9m of Bright light and 9m of Dim light beyond that
- All creatures of your choice within 9m of the card must make a constitution saving throw or take 2 rolls of your **Card Dice** in radiant damage and be [[Blinded]], or half as much on a successful throw
- You can concentrate on this activation to keep it active for up to a minute or when you are further than 9m away from the card
### Knight
You gain the following abilities:
- **Summon the Knight Spirit:** You may expend 1 **Deck Energy** to cast the [[Spirit Guardians]] spell, or expend 3 **Deck Energy** to cast it without using concentration (But it still lasts the full 10 Minutes)
- **Manifest the Knight Spirit:** You may expend 2 **Deck Energy** to cast the [[Summon Construct]] spell, or expend 4 **Deck Energy** to cast it without concentration

## Level 17: Dealer of Cards
Whenever you expend **Deck Energy** for an ability, if all the targets do one of the following:
- Fail their saving throw (Other than saves made for the *Sun* or *Knight* card)
- *With the Jester card* fail their D20 Test
- *With the Fool card* succeed their D20 Test
- Lose Concentration before your next turn after casting a spell
You may regain 1 **Deck Energy**
